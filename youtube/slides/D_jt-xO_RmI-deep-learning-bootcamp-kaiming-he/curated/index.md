## Curation Rule

The raw extraction contains 125 frames. This curated set keeps 37 frames that preserve the lecture's linear argument:

1. representation learning as the central problem;
2. image representations from LeNet to AlexNet and transfer learning;
3. depth and architectural scaling through VGG, Inception, normalization, and ResNet;
4. extension from images to sequences, Transformer, GPT, AlphaFold, and Vision Transformer.

The current images have been replaced with 1080p frames extracted from the YouTube `137` video stream. A small number of timestamps were manually shifted by a few seconds after the first pass, because exact slide content sometimes appears immediately after a camera-only shot.
